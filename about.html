How I bake.

Three simple rules, and a starter that’s older than Elouise’s favorite stuffed animal.

Slow, on purpose.

Every loaf gets a long, cold ferment. It takes longer, and it tastes like it.

Short ingredient list.

Flour, water, salt, and a well-fed starter. Anything extra comes from the garden or someone I know.

Small batches.

One home oven, a handful of loaves at a time. Each one shaped and scored by hand.
